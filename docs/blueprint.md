# **App Name**: FeastFlow

## Core Features:

- Personalized Offer Suggestions: Utilizes AI to analyze user's past orders and browsing behavior to tool personalized offers and promotions on the home screen.
- Dynamic Home Screen Layout: Displays location selector, search bar, offer slider, combo section, offers & coupons, categories, and popular products, all responsively adapting for mobile-first experience.
- Product Browsing and Details: Presents product cards with image, name, price, veg/non-veg indicator, and an 'add to cart' button. Clicking a product card navigates to a detailed view.
- Seamless Bottom Navigation: Provides intuitive navigation across Home, Menu, Orders, Rewards, and Profile sections with responsive tap feedback.
- Shopping Cart Management: Allows users to add/remove items to a temporary cart, view selected items, and proceed to checkout.
- Order History and Status: Enables users to view their past orders and check the real-time status of active deliveries.
- User Profile and Settings: Manages user personal information, delivery addresses, payment methods, and notification preferences.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: A vibrant, elegant scarlet red (#DC143C) chosen for its dynamic energy and appetite-stimulating qualities, complementing the app's premium feel on a light background. (HSL: 348, 83%, 47%)
- Background Color: A pristine, barely-there off-white (#FFF9F9) derived from the primary red's hue, providing a clean canvas that feels expansive and modern. (HSL: 0, 60%, 99%)
- Accent Color: A warm, inviting vermilion (#E75C41) with slightly less saturation and brightness than the primary, offering a complementary highlight that draws attention without overpowering. (HSL: 8, 77%, 58%)
- Headings and primary text: 'Inter', a contemporary sans-serif, chosen for its clear legibility, modern aesthetic, and versatility across various text sizes, ensuring a sleek, premium appearance. 
- Modern, outline-style icons with a consistent line weight. For product cards, 'veg' indicator icons should be clear, minimalist shapes (e.g., green circle). All icons should visually align with the 'smooth modern UI' concept.
- All content blocks, cards, and interactive elements should feature pronounced rounded corners and soft, subtle shadows (e.g., rgba(0,0,0,0.08) spread) to enhance the 'premium modern' and 'smooth' tactile feel, while ensuring mobile-first responsiveness.
- Incorporate smooth, subtle transitions for screen changes, loading states, and interactive elements like 'add to cart' buttons or navigation taps. Implement a soft slide animation for the offer banner slider and tasteful scaling/fading on card taps.
